A Letter From My Heart

My beautiful Smrithiii,

Yesterday, I let my emotions get the better of me, and I started a fight that hurt both of us.

Seeing you sad is the worst feeling for me. I deeply regret my actions, and I promise to become better.

You mean everything to me, and I never want to be the reason for your tears.

I love you more than words can express. There is nothing I regret more than being the reason your beautiful smile faded.

You are the most precious part of my life, and the thought of you being hurt because of me breaks my heart.

I promise to listen more carefully, understand you better, and protect your heart as fiercely as I love it.

My heart will always belong to you, today and forever. Please forgive me, my Smrithiii. ❤️

I Love You Forever
